Need to write a report?

A grant report doesn't have to be scary. Follow these three steps and you'll have a tidy one-pager ready to send.

1
Pull program outcomes
We'll gather every metric tagged to this grant — people served, sessions delivered, milestones met — straight from your Programs tab. You don't need to retype anything.
2
Match to funder priorities
Each funder cares about slightly different things. Tell us which grant you're reporting on and we'll line your outcomes up next to the goals from the original application.
3
Export a one-page summary
Generate a clean PDF with your numbers, a short narrative prompt for each metric, and your logo at the top. Edit before you send — or send as-is.
