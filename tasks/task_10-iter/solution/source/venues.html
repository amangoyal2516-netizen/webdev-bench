Riverside Pavilion

A glass-walled civic pavilion on the embankment, opened in 2001 and used for seasonal markets, mayoral receptions, and the annual Solstice Long Table since 2003.

 Accessibility

  • Step-free ramp at all public entrances
  • Service elevator to mezzanine kitchen
  • Hearing-induction loop (front of house)

  • Tactile signage and braille wayfinding

How requests are reviewed

Venue requests are received by the Food & Culture Office and routed to the borough steward within 5 working days. Civic, non-commercial, and accessibility-forward programs are prioritised under Resolution 2017–C/14.
