The Mill Pond Supper: A Harvest Banquet for the Northbridge Ward

A six-course civic dinner served to one hundred and twenty residents on the eve of the autumn harvest, prepared with produce sourced exclusively from growers within forty miles of the ward boundary.

Date served   Saturday, 12 October 2024 Venue   Northbridge Community Hall, 412 Mill Pond Rd. Presiding chef   Inés Marchetti-Okonkwo Attendance   118 of 120 seats filed
Record № 2024-038 Filed by Clerk H. Vasquez-Lin Archived 21 Oct 2024 Series Harvest Suppers, Vol. IX Status Verified

Bill of Fare

Six Courses · Served Family Style
  1. 01
    Mill Pond Greens with Pressed Apple & Toasted Buckwheat

    Bitter chicories and tender butterhead from the Northbridge Allotments, dressed with cold-pressed Honeycrisp vinegar, finished with cracked buckwheat and shaved sheep's milk pecorino.

    VEG GF
  2. 02
    Smoked Trout on Rye, Cured Beet & Horseradish Cream

    Cold-smoked rainbow trout from the Eastfork Hatchery on dark caraway rye baked that morning at Vasilenko Bakery, with house-cured chioggia beet and fresh-grated horseradish.

    FISH DAIRY
  3. 03
    Squash & Chestnut Velouté, Sage Oil

    A silken purée of Long Island Cheese pumpkin and roasted chestnut, finished tableside with a thread of sage-infused sunflower oil and toasted pepitas.

    VGN GF
  4. 04
    Slow-Braised Ward Lamb with Quince & Barley

    Pasture-raised shoulder from Halberg Farm braised six hours in dark cider, served over hulled barley with poached quince and bitter herbs from the school garden plot.

    LAMB
  5. 05
    Stone-Ground Cornbread, Cultured Butter, Sorghum Drizzle

    Whole-kernel cornbread baked in cast iron from Otsego heritage maize, served warm with cultured butter from the Riverbend Dairy Co-op and a thread of dark sorghum.

    VEG DAIRY
  6. 06
    Pear Tart with Walnut Crumb & Buttermilk Ice

    Bartlett pears poached in spiced cider laid into a black walnut shortbread, served with buttermilk granita and a single candied walnut from the Mill Pond grove.

    VEG DAIRY

End of Bill of Fare · Six (6) courses recorded · Service commenced 18:30, concluded 21:45.

Chef's Note

Every autumn I return to the question of what a public meal owes the ground it stands on. The Northbridge Ward grows quietly — its allotments tucked behind the old textile sheds, its orchard mostly forgotten until October. This menu is a letter of thanks to the twenty-three growers, millers, and makers who fed one hundred and eighteen of their neighbours on a single Saturday evening. I asked the kitchen to leave the seasonings light. The land was loud enough.

— Inés Marchetti-Okonkwo, Presiding Chef

Allergen Disclosure

This menu was prepared in a kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts (including black walnut), and sesame. Course 02 contains fish and gluten; Course 04 contains lamb and barley; Course 06 contains tree nuts and dairy. Two seats were filed under nut-free accommodation and received a substituted dessert of poached pear with buttermilk ice only. No incidents of allergic reaction were reported during or after service. Filed in accordance with §7-204(c) of the Public Meals Code.
