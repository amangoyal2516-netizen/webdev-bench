Scope 2 — Location vs Market

Section 3.2 · GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance
Generated narrative AI-drafted · review required

Northwind Foods Co. reports Scope 2 emissions for FY2024 using both the location-based and market-based methods as required by the GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance (2015). The operational boundary covers 18 owned and leased facilities across the EU-27, the United Kingdom, and the United States, totalling 142.4 GWh of purchased electricity and 31.2 GWh of district heating.

Under the location-based method, gross Scope 2 emissions were 3,612 tCO₂e, a −8.4% year-on-year reduction driven by grid decarbonisation in the IE and DK service territories. Under the market-based method, residual emissions after the retirement of 96,400 MWh of bundled GO certificates and 12,000 MWh of vPPA-sourced energy were 3,204 tCO₂e.

Contractual instruments meet the eight Scope 2 quality criteria; supporting documentation is filed in the Ledger under tags scope2-2024 and go-retired.
