The beginner path

A chronological journey from your first straight cut to a finished side table. Twelve weeks, twelve small kits, three checkpoints. Each kit ships with pre-milled stock, a printed plan, and the exact tools you'll need next — no guesswork, no half-bought toolboxes.

Checkpoint · W04

You can cut. Cleanly.

Four kits in, you've moved from square cuts to compound tapers. Before joining anything together, take an evening to drill the basics until they feel boring.

Skills you should own
  • Straight crosscuts to a knife line
  • Setting and using a block plane
  • Chopping a notch in three strokes
  • Sanding without flat spots or swirls

Checkpoint · W08

You can join wood to wood.

Halfway through. You've cut, doweled, lapped, mortised and dovetailed. Now the work shifts from "does it hold?" to "does it look like you meant it?"

Skills you should own
  • Reading a joint before you cut it
  • Cutting on the waste side, every time
  • A four-corner glue-up under 8 minutes
  • Recovering from a slightly fat tenon
