Before you start

Three things, every session

Print the safety checklist

Eye protection

Safety glasses on before any saw, chisel or drill touches wood. Chips travel further than you think — especially with end-grain.

Required · every week

Sharp edges

Both hands always behind the cutting edge. A sharp tool is a safe tool — dull blades slip. Cap chisels when you set them down.

First introduced · Week 02

Dust

Sanding, drilling and any oily rag work needs ventilation and a mask. Soak finishing rags in water before binning — they can self-ignite.
