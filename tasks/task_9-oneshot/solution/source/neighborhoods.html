Featured · San Francisco

Dogpatch & the Eastern Shoreline

Once a shipyard outpost stitched to the rest of the city by streetcar lines and grain silos, Dogpatch has spent the last decade rebuilding itself around its own contradictions — Victorian cottages beside concrete loft conversions, a working dry dock two blocks from a tasting room. The result is one of the few districts where supply and demand still genuinely argue with one another.
