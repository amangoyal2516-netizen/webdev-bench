Key hygiene checklist
  • Store sk_* in a server-side secret manager — never ship to the browser.
  • Pair each environment (sandbox, staging, live) with its own dedicated key.
  • Rotate every 90 days or immediately after a teammate offboards.
  • Restrict scopes to the minimum your service actually needs.
