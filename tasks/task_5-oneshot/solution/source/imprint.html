Pitch us a cookbook

We read every proposal that reaches us through the submissions form. We publish four to six titles a year and prefer voices we have not heard before.

  1. One — The shape of itSend a one-page letter describing the book, who it's for, and why you are the cook to write it.
  2. Two — Three recipesInclude three sample recipes in your own voice, with headnotes. Loose drafts are welcome.
  3. Three — A table of contentsSketch the chapters and roughly how many recipes each will hold. A working title helps.
