SP-185 · meditation

Body scan — settle in before the standup

meditation beginner-friendly guided audio
Status

CategoryMeditation · seated Duration10 minutes RecurrenceWeekdays · 08:30 Difficulty★★☆☆☆ Gentle Streak4 of 5 this week GuideTara Mendoza
Guided steps
  1. 1Sit comfortably. Feet flat, hands resting. Let your eyes soften or close.
  2. 2Take three slow breaths — inhale through the nose, exhale a little longer than the inhale.
  3. 3Bring attention to the crown of your head. Notice any sensation — warmth, pressure, nothing at all is fine.
  4. 4Slowly travel down: face, jaw, shoulders, chest, belly, hips, legs, feet. About 30 seconds at each stop.
  5. 5Rest in the whole body for a final breath. Open your eyes when you're ready.
